About

A little about me

I'm Christian Oyaman, a graduate of the Bachelor of Science in Information Systems and Development program, with a strong interest in web development and technology. I enjoy turning ideas into interfaces — figuring out how a layout should breathe, how a button should feel to click, and how a page should read on a phone just as well as it does on a desktop.

My focus right now is the frontend: structuring pages with semantic HTML, styling them with modern CSS, and bringing them to life with vanilla JavaScript. Alongside that, I'm building a working understanding of backend concepts like PHP and MySQL, so I can follow a project from interface to data.

After completing my education, I'm now applying what I learned to real projects and continuing to grow as a developer.
